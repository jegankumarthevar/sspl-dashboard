Honest limits

  • Wallet-level buys are parsed only from recent transactions touching the selected liquidity pool. Wallet independence and historical performance remain UNVERIFIED or INSUFFICIENT SAMPLE without a wallet-history source.
  • Market price or volume does not prove wallet purchases. This app qualifies only when transaction-level buy evidence is parsed.
  • The browser cannot scan after this page is closed. Browser notification, sound, local storage, and background timers vary by browser and OS.
  • Public endpoints can rate-limit, fail, or return incomplete data. Failed mandatory checks produce DATA INCOMPLETE, not a signal.
  • Telegram is not included because a bot token must never be pasted into public HTML. Sending Telegram would require a separate trusted delivery service.
  • 1.5x/2x/3x observations are not guaranteed returns and may not be executable after slippage, fees, or low liquidity.
